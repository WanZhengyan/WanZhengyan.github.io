Corrected Samplers for Discrete Flow Models  [Code]
Zhengyan Wan, Yidong Ouyang, Liyan Xie, Hongyuan Zha†, Fang Fang†, Guang Cheng
ICML, 2026

We propose two corrected samplers: time-corrected sampler and location-corrected sampler. We establish a one-step lower bound for the Euler sampler and non-asymptotic upper bounds for the proposed corrected samplers, showing that the location-corrected sampler achieves lower iteration complexity than the Euler sampler.
Error Analysis of Discrete Flow with Generator Matching
Zhengyan Wan, Yidong Ouyang, Qiang Yao, Liyan Xie, Fang Fang†, Hongyuan Zha†, Guang Cheng
ICML, 2026

We establish non-asymptotic error bounds for distribution error in discrete flow models by taking approximation error into account. Both fast and slow convergence rates are derived with and without the lower-boundedness condition, respectively.
Discrete Guidance Matching: Exact Guidance for Discrete Flow Matching  [Code]
Zhengyan Wan*, Yidong Ouyang*, Liyan Xie, Fang Fang†, Hongyuan Zha†, Guang Cheng
ICLR, 2026

We derive the exact transition rate for the desired distribution given a learned discrete flow matching model, leading to guidance that only requires a single forward pass in each sampling step, significantly improving sampling efficiency.
